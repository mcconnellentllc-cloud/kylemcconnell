Kyle McConnell

I farm and ranch near Haxtun, Colorado, on the place my family started four generations back.

I left for a while. Junior college, then Fort Hays State. A grain desk in Iowa, a stretch selling real estate in Phoenix. I came home in 2009 and have been here since. Brandi and I are raising our family on the place.

We sell Pioneer seed and run a few ag businesses alongside the farm. I put together the websites and tools we use and keep them running.
