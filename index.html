Haxtun, Colorado

Kyle McConnell

Farmer. Rancher. Business owner. Self-taught developer. Songwriter.

I am the fourth generation of my family to farm and ranch in northeast Colorado. I left Haxtun for a while — junior college, Fort Hays State, a grain desk in Iowa, a real estate license in Phoenix — and came back to the farm in 2009.

Today I farm and ranch, sell Pioneer seed, run a handful of ag businesses, and write the software all of them depend on. Nobody taught me to code. I started building because the tools our operation needed did not exist.

The long way around

Haxtun, and everywhere between leaving and coming back.

  1. 2000

    Haxtun High School

    Graduated. Class president, H Club president, and FFA chapter president that year.

  2. 2001–2002

    Northeastern Junior College

    Two years at NJC before heading north.

  3. 2002

    Montana, then Hays

    Worked a season in Montana, moved back to Hays, Kansas, and drove truck for Befort Harvesting.

  4. 2003–2005

    Fort Hays State University

    Returned to FHSU in 2003, competed on the livestock judging team, and graduated in 2005.

  5. After graduation

    Carroll, Iowa

    Grain originator for West Central Coop at Templeton.

  6. Next

    Phoenix, Arizona

    Earned a real estate license and worked in the realty business.

  7. Back home

    Grainland Coop, Haxtun

    Fertilizer and chemical sales, grain origination, and sprayer operator.

  8. 2009

    Home to the family farm

    Returned to the operation for good.

  9. August 4, 2012

    Married Brandi

    We raise our family on the farm.

  10. Today

    Farming, seed, and software

    Farming and ranching, Pioneer seed, farm-fresh beef, JD Skiles and other ag inputs, Acre Profit for wholesale chemicals, and M77 AG.
